Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

The Wedding Desk is a wedding planning app run by [Business legal name] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • Your wedding plan is saved on your own device unless you choose to sign in and share it.
  • There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics in the app, and we never sell or rent your information.
  • Vendor logins and passwords you save in the app stay on your device. They are never uploaded, exported to Excel or PDF, or shown to anyone you share with.
  • If you buy from the Stationery shop, payment is handled by Stripe. We never see your full card number.

Information you put in the app

You may enter wedding details, tasks, budget and payments, vendors, and guest names with phone numbers, emails, addresses, meals and RSVPs. Without sign-in, all of this is stored only in your browser's storage on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us.

When you text, email or call a guest or vendor from the app, the message is written for you and then opened in your own phone's messaging, email or phone app. It is sent by you, from your account, not by us.

You are responsible for having the right to keep your guests' contact details, and for using them only for your wedding.

If you sign in and share your plan

Signing in is optional. If you sign in, we collect your email address, the name you give, and your role in the wedding (for example Bride, Groom or Maid of Honor). Your shared plan is stored with our database provider, Supabase, so everyone you invite can see the same plan. Sign-in codes are sent to your email by our email provider.

Only people you invite can see your plan, at the access level you give them (owner, editor or viewer). Vendor logins and passwords are removed before anything is uploaded.

If you buy stationery

Orders are paid through Stripe's secure checkout. Stripe collects your payment details, name, email, and shipping address, and handles them under its own privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy. We receive your order details, contact and shipping information, and the personalization you enter (such as names and wedding date) so we can make and ship your order. We keep order records as long as needed for taxes, accounting and customer service.

Hosting and technical data

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information such as IP addresses to keep the service running and secure. The app does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

Who we share information with

Only the service providers named above (Supabase, our email provider, Stripe and GitHub), and only to run the app and fill orders. We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • On your device: in Settings, tap Start My Own Wedding to clear the example, or clear your browser's site data to remove everything.
  • Signed in: email us at [contact email] to get a copy of your information or to have your account and shared plan deleted. We respond within 30 days.

Children

The Wedding Desk is meant for adults planning a wedding. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), row-level access rules on shared plans, and keep sensitive vendor logins on your device only. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your device locked and your email account protected.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let signed-in users know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: [contact email].
